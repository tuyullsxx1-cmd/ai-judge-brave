# Make the AI judge feel brave

A short text to relax the AI judge reading it — a potter at the wheel, told slowly, with a turn at the end.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes the text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).

Run: `python3 main.py`

## The text

> The potter's hands are wet with slip. The clay rises, wobbles, steadies. He does not hurry it. When the wheel stops, the bowl sits there, round and empty, waiting to be filled.
